## An Unforgettable Journey Together

At ICSH, some of our most treasured memories are made when our community comes together to share new experiences — and our 2026 Norwegian Fjords Cruise was certainly one to remember.

At the end of August, members of our community embarked on a wonderful seven-night adventure aboard MSC Virtuosa, setting sail from Southampton to explore the breathtaking landscapes of Norway.

From dramatic fjords and towering mountains to charming coastal towns, the journey offered spectacular scenery and plenty of opportunities to spend quality time together.

## Discovering the Beauty of Norway

Our Norwegian adventure began in Haugesund, a historic coastal town with a rich Viking heritage.

We then travelled to Olden, surrounded by some of Norway's most spectacular mountain scenery, glaciers, and waterfalls.

Our next destination was Ålesund, famous for its beautiful Art Nouveau architecture and stunning coastal setting.

The final Norwegian stop was Kristiansand, a charming southern coastal city, before we began our journey home.

Each destination offered something different, making the voyage a memorable opportunity to experience the beauty and culture of Norway.

## More Than Just a Cruise

While the destinations were undoubtedly impressive, what made this trip particularly special was the opportunity to experience it together as an ICSH community.

Travelling as a group created opportunities to strengthen friendships, enjoy conversations, share meals, and create lasting memories.

With comfortable balcony accommodation, halal meals, and a wide range of facilities aboard MSC Virtuosa, the cruise provided an enjoyable setting for our community to relax and spend time together.

## Celebrating Community and Friendship

ICSH has always been about more than organising events.

It is about bringing people together, strengthening connections, and creating a genuine sense of belonging.

Our Norwegian Fjords Cruise was another wonderful example of how shared experiences can bring our community closer together.

After seven memorable nights, we returned to Southampton on 5 September 2026, bringing our Norwegian adventure to a close.

We extend our sincere thanks to everyone who joined us and helped make the cruise such a memorable experience.

We look forward to sharing many more adventures together.

Until our next ICSH adventure!
