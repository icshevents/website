# Automatic paid membership activation

Owner approved automatic activation on 23 October 2026. A verified live membership payment now activates the matching Bigin Contact, marks Payment Status Completed, and sets Membership Date and Expiry Date to twelve calendar months from the UK date of payment. The GBP 1 Test/Test condition is a real payment and follows this rule. Free memberships still require ICSH confirmation.

Stripe's signed webhook first verifies and durably records payment, then runs activation. The private activation ledger binds one payment intent, paid timestamp, fixed dates, contact ID and a three-minute claim. Delivery retries read the remote fields before any repeat PUT; an already matching term completes without writing again or extending the dates. Unknown provider outcomes remain retryable. Another current membership term, identity conflict, ambiguous contact, invalid existing dates or expired paid term requires organiser review. Refunds and disputes are not automatically turned into membership revocation; their payment status continues to require review.

Contact matching checks full name, email and phone; a stored Contact ID is also verified. An existing contact can be located for a renewal only after exact matching. Writes contain only Membership_Status, Payment_Status, Membership_Date and Expiry_Date, with trigger: [] to avoid invoking unrelated workflows. Contact details, communication consent and the existing Zoho Forms → Stripe connection are unchanged. Activation is recorded as complete only after remote readback confirms all four fields and identity.

ZOHO_BIGIN_MEMBERSHIP_ACTIVATION_ENABLED gates new activation attempts. The existing approved lookup grant is reused and needs Contact UPDATE as well as READ/SEARCH permissions. No Stripe credentials are changed. The private CRON_SECRET protects /api/cron/membership-activation. Webhook failures cause Stripe retries; a daily recovery job at 02:00 UTC also processes up to five eligible pending paid entries. Approved organisers can retry from Membership entries. Identity/term conflicts are held for review rather than retried blindly.

Deletion blocks new activation claims. If a payment is refunded or an application deleted while a remote write is in progress, completion is held for review. The activation ledger retains the payment and term audit. Private service-only SQL functions enforce paid-only eligibility, account isolation, lease ownership and public access denial.
